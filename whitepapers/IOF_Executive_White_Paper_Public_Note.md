# IOF Executive White Paper — Public Note

The supplied **Infinite Optical Fabric (IOF)** executive white paper describes a speculative post-von Neumann computing architecture based on photonic manifolds, resonant modes, and topological metaphors. It is written for investors, engineers, and policy makers and proposes a research direction rather than documenting a validated product.

Several statements in the supplied source require a clear evidence boundary before they can be presented as results. In particular, claims of mathematically eliminated dissipation, a 50% AI energy-waste baseline, completed FDTD proof, zero-terminal loss, infinite scalability, and a Q-factor above 10⁸ are not established by the source text alone. The document should use terms such as **proposed**, **target**, **hypothesis**, and **validation roadmap** unless it is accompanied by reproducible simulations, raw outputs, measurement conditions, and independent review.

For the complete supplied text, see the [archival source](archive/IOF_Executive_White_Paper_Supplied.md). For a claim-by-claim assessment and safer wording, see the [Editorial and Evidence Review](IOF_Editorial_and_Evidence_Review.md) and the [publication-safe executive brief](IOF_Public_Executive_Brief.md).
