PegaConstellation · Gregory Scott Davis

Researching systems that resonate, adapt, and endure.

PegaConstellation is an independent open research program spanning resonant and photonic computing, endpoint resilience, geometric substrates, and human–AI systems. The work is exploratory: public claims are separated from the measurements and implementation evidence still being developed.
